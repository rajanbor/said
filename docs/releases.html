What's new in Said

To update, run the install command again. Technical details are in the changelog.

Said 0.4.3

18 November 2026

  • Each new recording starts with the pill back at the bottom of the screen, wherever you dragged it last time.

Said 0.4.2

18 November 2026

  • You can grab the recording pill and drag it wherever you want again. It stays there next time.

Said 0.4.1

18 November 2026

  • Links from other apps or web pages can no longer start a recording by themselves. Said now asks you first.
  • When recording a call, Said records only that call's sound. If it can't find it, it records just your microphone instead of everything your Mac plays.
  • If saving goes wrong, you no longer lose the recording. Said keeps the raw tracks.
  • Your recordings folder is now private to you. Other people using this Mac can't open it.
  • The installer notices if a release was tampered with after you installed it, and stops.

Said 0.4.0

18 November 2026

  • Said speaks English, and Polish for Polish systems. It follows your Mac's language.

Said 0.3.1

18 November 2026

  • Forgot to stop? After 2 minutes of silence you get a notification with a stop button.
  • Said warns you when your disk is getting full, and saves the recording before space runs out.
  • Recording a call needs far less free space: the call audio takes about 45 MB per hour instead of over 1.3 GB.
  • A simpler menu that looks like the rest of macOS, with a button that opens your recordings folder.

Said 0.3.0

18 November 2026

  • Call recordings now include the other people, not just you, even when you wear headphones.
  • You can also record everything your Mac plays, if you turn it on in the menu.

Said 0.2.0

18 November 2026

  • When a call starts in Zoom, Teams, Google Meet and others, Said asks if you want to record it, and stops when the call ends.
  • Your Mac remembers that Said may use the microphone, even after updates.

Said 0.1.0

18 November 2026

  • First version: record from the menu bar, a small pill while recording, and a text transcript made on your Mac.
